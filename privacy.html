Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 19, 2026

Overview

IMAN is built with your privacy in mind. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not show you ads. Most of your data never leaves your device.

This policy explains what information the IMAN app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using IMAN, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Location — IMAN requests access to your device's location while the app is in use. This is used solely to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Your location is never stored on our servers and is never shared with third parties.

Prayer logs & streaks — Your daily prayer records, streak count, and XP are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your phone and is not uploaded to any server.

Profile information — Your chosen name and avatar (emoji) are stored locally on your device. You can change or delete this at any time from your profile settings.

Community posts — If you choose to post in the community feed, your display name, avatar, and post content are stored on our secure servers so other users can see them. You can delete your posts at any time.

App settings — Your chosen language, calculation method, madhab, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Real names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Payment or financial information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history or cross-app activity
  • Precise location history — location is used in the moment and discarded

Subscription & Payments

IMAN offers a 10-day free trial, after which a subscription is required to continue using the app. Two plans are available:

  • Monthly — $4.00 USD per month
  • Yearly — $29.99 USD per year (save 38%)

Subscriptions are managed and billed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information — all billing is handled entirely by Apple.

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No refunds are issued for partial months or partial years.

Third-Party Services

Supabase — Community posts and replies are stored using Supabase, a secure database platform. Only content you choose to share publicly is stored there. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Al-Quran Cloud API — The Quran screen fetches surah and ayah data from the public Al-Quran Cloud API. No personal information is sent in these requests.

Apple — Subscriptions and push notifications are handled through Apple's infrastructure. Apple's privacy policy applies to these services.

We do not use any analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services that collect personal data.

Permissions

Location (When In Use) — Used to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. You may deny this; prayer times will not be available without it.

Notifications — Used to send prayer time reminders. Optional. You can enable or disable this at any time in Settings.

Motion & Orientation (iOS) — Used by the Qibla compass. This data is used in real time only and is never stored or transmitted.

Data Retention & Deletion

Local data can be deleted by uninstalling the app or using Reset Profile in your profile screen. Community posts can be deleted at any time from within the app. Deleted posts are permanently removed from our servers.

Children's Privacy

IMAN is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the community feature, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
